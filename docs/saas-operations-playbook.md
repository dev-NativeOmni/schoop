# SaaS Operations Playbook

## Role Internal

- Super admin: akses penuh.
- Operations manager: subscription, billing, onboarding, support, incident, release.
- Support staff: support ticket, incident terbatas, knowledge base.
- Customer success: onboarding, support, health score, customer notes.
- Sales: pipeline implementasi dan informasi komersial terbatas.

## Daily Checklist

- Cek open critical/high support ticket.
- Jalankan atau cek hasil SLA breach checker.
- Review incident open.
- Review tenant health critical/risk.
- Pastikan backup dan health check OK.

## Weekly Checklist

- Review onboarding project yang tertahan.
- Review invoice tenant overdue.
- Review release note draft/published.
- Review knowledge base artikel baru.
- Customer success follow-up untuk tenant risk.

## Monthly Checklist

- Review subscription renewal.
- Review support volume per sekolah.
- Review health score trend.
- Review incident postmortem.
- Review legal/compliance checklist.
